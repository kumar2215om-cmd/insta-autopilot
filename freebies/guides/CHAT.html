Kya milega

ChatLLM ek subscription mein bahut saare AI models deta hai: chat ke liye ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini jaise models, image aur video models, ek app-building AI Agent, aur slides. Plan $10/user/month se shuru hota hai (Basic). Ye guide batati hai kya milta hai, kya nahi, aur credits kaise bachayein.

Link: chatllm.abacus.ai: sign up se pehle pricing page zaroor dekho, kyunki plans aur models badalte rehte hain.

4 features, kaise use karein

  1. RouteLLM: model dropdown mein RouteLLM chuno. Har prompt ke liye ye khud model chunta hai (writing, coding, maths ke liye alag). Kisi specific model ka answer chahiye to dropdown se manually chuno.
  2. Image + Video: sidebar mein Image/Video kholo, model chuno, prompt likho ya product photo upload karo. UGC-style ad ke liye prompt: "Ek 20-25 saal ki creator is product ko haath mein pakad ke review kar rahi hai, natural light, phone-style video, 8 seconds."
  3. Abacus AI Agent: app describe karo (screens, login, database, payments). Agent banake deploy karta hai. Payments ke liye apna Stripe account jodna padega. Live karne se pehle khud test karo.
  4. Slides: ek prompt do ("10-slide investor deck, real charts ke saath"), phir Export: PDF, PPTX ya Google Slides.

Mera honest review

Achha: - Ek jagah pe kai top models: alag-alag tabs aur bills nahi - RouteLLM rozana ke kaam ke liye convenient hai - Image, video, slides aur app-builder ek hi plan mein

Dhyan rakho: - Credits-based hai, unlimited nahi. Video aur agent bahut credits khaate hain. - Har model ka har feature (jaise official apps ke kuch tools) yahan same nahi milta. - Kling, Seedance, Veo jaise models ki availability badalti rehti hai: sign up se pehle list check karo. - Agent se bani app production mein daalne se pehle security aur payments khud test karo.

Kiske liye best: jo log 2-3 AI subscriptions le rahe hain aur mostly chat, writing, research aur kabhi-kabhi images/slides use karte hain.
