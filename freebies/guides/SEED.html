Credits bachane ke tips

  • Pehle short clip: 5 second ki video se prompt test karo, sahi lage tab lambi banao.
  • Low resolution pehle: draft kam quality mein, final hi high quality mein.
  • Roz use karo: daily credits aksar jama nahi hote; jo aaj ke hain, aaj use karo.
  • Prompt ek baar mein pakka: neeche wala template bharo, baar-baar regenerate nahi karna padega.
Prompt template
[Shot type: drone / close-up / wide], [subject + action], [location], [time of day + light], [mood], [camera move: slow push-in / pan left], cinematic, [aspect ratio 9:16], [audio: ambient sound / music style]

Example: drone shot, ek akela traveller pahaad ki choti pe khada, golden hour sunset, peaceful mood, slow push-in, cinematic, 9:16, soft wind sound.

Bacho

In "unlimited free tricks" se bacho

  • Fake / multiple accounts: app ke terms todte hain; account aur credits dono ban ho sakte hain.
  • Leaked API keys: kisi aur ki key use karna galat hai, aur aksar wo key band ho jaati hai.
  • "Unlimited" mod apps / APK: inme malware ka khatra hota hai; sirf official app ya website use karo.
  • Paid course ya "secret trick" bechne wale: jo free hai, wo upar ki list mein hai.
