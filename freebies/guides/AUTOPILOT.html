Claude se Instagram autopilot (safe setup)

Claude tumhara Instagram dekhe, niche ke top creators aur is hafte ke topics padhe, product reels banaye, aur comments ko leads mein badle. 4 steps.

Ye kya hai

Connect → Creators → Topics → Posts

Tumne AUTOPILOT comment kiya tha, to ye raha poora setup. 🙌 Idea: Claude ko tumhara Instagram dikhao, wo niche ke top creators aur is hafte ke topics padhe, tumhare product ke liye reels banaye, aur comments ko leads mein badle.

⚠️ Honest + safe note: Instagram connection Ootto (third-party connector) se hota hai, Claude ka official feature nahi. Har reel post hone se pehle khud approve karo; fully automatic posting aur replies robotic lag sakte hain. Ek account pe consistency rakho: bahut saare accounts se same product push karna ya mass automated DMs/comments Instagram ke spam rules todte hain, aur account restrict ya ban ho sakta hai. Reel mein dikhaye leads ek example the.
Step 1

Connect

  1. Instagram account Professional (Creator ya Business) hona chahiye.
  2. claude.ai → Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  3. Name: Ootto. URL: Ootto ki official website se copy karo.
  4. Connect → Instagram login → permissions padho → Allow. "Connected" dikhe to done.
Step 2

Creators

Copy karo
Mere niche [niche] ke top 5 creators dhoondo jo is hafte sabse zyada chal rahe hain.
Har ek ki top 3 reels ke hooks todo: pehle 3 second mein kya dikha, kya bola.
Table mein do: creator, reel, views, hook type.
Step 3

Topics → product reel

Copy karo
Is hafte mere niche mein kya sach mein chal raha hai (topics + formats jo saves la rahe hain)?
Unmein se 3 topics chuno jo mere product [product + 1 line] se jud sakte hain.
Har ek ke liye reel banao: hook (pehle 2 sec), 30-40 sec script, on-screen text,
caption aur comment keyword. Pehle mujhe dikhao, post mat karna.
Step 4

Posts + comments → leads

Copy karo
Maine Day 1 reel approve kar di hai. Ise publish karo.
Phir comments ke reply drafts likho: short, friendly, Hinglish.
Jo bhi price, link ya "kaise" pooche, unhe LEAD mark karo aur DM ka draft do.
Koi bhi DM bhejne se pehle mujhe dikhao.
💡 Comment → DM automatic chahiye? Iske liye ManyChat jaisa approved Instagram tool use karo: trigger = keyword comment, action = DM mein link. Ye Meta ke rules ke andar chalta hai.
Routine

Roz ka routine (15 min)

  • Aaj ki reel padho aur approve karo
  • Kal ke comments ke reply drafts check karke bhejo
  • LEAD wale logon ki list dekho, khud follow-up karo
  • Hafte mein ek baar: "kya chala, kya nahi" poochho aur agle hafte ka plan update karo
