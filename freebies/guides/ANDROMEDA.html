What is it

What is Andromeda?

Andromeda is Meta's AI-powered ad retrieval system, announced by Meta Engineering in December 2024. Before the ad auction, it picks a few thousand candidate ads for each person out of tens of millions. It was built because generative AI made ad volume explode (Meta said advertisers created 15 million+ ads with its GenAI tools in a single month), and the old system couldn't keep up.

💡 What it means for you: Andromeda decides who sees your ad mostly by understanding your creative. So the creative now does the targeting.
3 changes

The 3 big changes

1 · Creative = targeting. Meta reads your video, image and text to work out who the ad is for. Different creatives reach different people automatically, so you don't need tight interest targeting anymore.

2 · Real creative diversity. Small tweaks (a new colour, the same video with a new headline) look like the same ad to Andromeda. You need ads that are genuinely different:

  • Hook: a different first 3 seconds or opening line
  • Format: video vs static vs carousel vs UGC vs founder-to-camera
  • Angle: problem, benefit, social proof, comparison, offer, story

3 · Simple structure, broad audience. Fewer campaigns and ad sets, with a broad audience, so all the learning data goes to one place.

Structure

A simple campaign structure (starter)

Campaign structure
Campaign: 1 · Sales (Advantage+ or manual CBO)
  Ad set: 1 · Broad (country, age 18–65+, no interests)
    Ads: 8–15 truly different creatives
      - 3 hooks × 2–3 formats × 2 angles
Budget: enough for ~50 conversions/week if possible
Rule: don't touch it for 3–7 days. Then cut losers, keep winners, add 3–5 new concepts every week.

Creative diversity checklist

  • At least 3 different hooks
  • At least 2 different formats (e.g. video and static)
  • At least 2 different angles
  • Different people or faces and settings where possible
  • A clear offer and CTA in every ad

Remember

Remember
