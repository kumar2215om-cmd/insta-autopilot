Privacy policy

{{ brand }} helps Instagram creators reply to comments and send direct messages automatically. This policy explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram account: account ID, username, name, profile picture, follower count and an access token from Instagram's official login. We never see your password.
  • Your settings: keyword rules, reply texts, DM texts and links you write.
  • Activity: the last 500 automation events (for example the commenter's username, the comment text and whether a DM was sent), so you can see what happened. Older events are removed automatically.
  • Counts: how many DMs, replies and links were sent.
  • Contacts: the Instagram usernames of people who interact with your automations, and the email address or phone number they choose to send in the DM when your automation asks for it, or through the signup form on your link page. Only you can see and export them.
  • Link clicks and sales: when someone taps a link in a DM, we count the click and keep a small cookie on their browser for 30 days (which DM link they tapped, and a random visitor ID). If they later pay on a creator's payment page that sends them to a {{ brand }} sales link, the sale (product, price, which automation) is credited to that DM. We don't receive their payment or card details.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the push address your browser gives us, so we can alert you about leads, sales and account problems.
  • AI Inbox (only if you turn on AI replies): the last 40 messages of each DM conversation, so the AI can answer in context and you can read them in the inbox.

How we use it

Only to run the automation you set up: reading comments and messages on your account, replying, sending DMs and checking whether someone follows you before sending your link. We don't sell data, show ads or use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

On Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (database), service providers that process data on our behalf. If you turn on AI replies, the DM conversation and the details you give the AI (your FAQ, products and links) are sent to Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model, to write each reply. Anthropic processes it on our behalf and doesn't use it to train its models.

If you turn on Integrations, new leads (username, email or phone, the automation) are also sent to the webhook address you choose, such as your own Google Sheet or Zapier. That service then handles them under its own terms.

Your choices

Use Disconnect Instagram and delete my data in the dashboard, or remove {{ brand }} in Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). Either way we delete your account data. See Data deletion.

Refund and cancellation policy

Cancellation

Paid plans are prepaid for one month or one year and don't renew automatically, so there's no subscription to cancel: your plan simply ends on its end date, shown on the Plan & billing page, and your account moves to the Free plan. You can stop using {{ brand }} or delete your account at any time.

Refunds

  • First payment: if {{ brand }} isn't right for you, ask for a full refund within 7 days of your first paid plan purchase.
  • Charged twice, or money taken for a failed payment: refunded in full.
  • Other payments (renewals, and any payment after its first 7 days) aren't refundable. Unused time isn't refunded, but switching plans turns it into extra days on the new plan.
