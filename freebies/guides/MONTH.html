Claude se 30 reels: poora setup

Claude ko Ootto connector se apne Instagram se jodo, phir ek line bolo: "Mere brand ke liye 30 reels banao." Pehle research, phir har reel.

Ye kya hai

Ye doc kya hai?

Tumne MONTH comment kiya tha, to ye raha poora setup. 🙌 Idea: Claude ko Ootto connector se apne Instagram se jodo, phir ek line bolo: "Mere brand ke liye 30 reels banao." Ye pehle research karta hai (competitors, niche, tumhara brand), phir usi research se har reel likhta hai.

⚠️ Honest note: Ootto ek third-party connector hai, Claude ka official Instagram feature nahi; connect karne se pehle permissions padho. Done-for-you (har mahine 30 reels bhejna) Ootto ki paid service hai, meri nahi; pricing aur reels ki quality khud check karo. "Har reel bana ke schedule karna" Ootto ka claim hai; pehle 2–3 reels khud dekh ke hi auto-schedule pe bharosa karo.
Step 1

Ootto ko Claude se jodo (ek baar)

  1. Instagram account Professional (Creator ya Business) hona chahiye.
  2. claude.ai kholo → Settings → Connectors.
  3. Add custom connector dabao.
  4. Name: Ootto. Remote MCP server URL: Ootto ki official website se link copy karke paste karo. Kisi random DM ya comment wala link mat use karo.
  5. Add → Connect → Ootto mein sign in → Instagram account choose karo → Allow.
  6. Connectors list mein Ootto ✓ Connected dikhe to done.
💡 Custom connectors har Claude plan pe available nahi hote; option na dikhe to apna plan check karo.
Step 2

Wo ek line

Naya chat kholo, confirm karo ki Ootto connected hai, aur ye bhejo:

Copy karo
Mere brand ke liye 30 reels banao.
Brand: @[handle] | Niche: [topic] | Audience: [kaun] | Language: Hinglish
Pehle research karo:
1. Mere 3-5 competitors ki top reels (hooks, formats)
2. Mere niche mein last 30 din ke outliers (average nahi)
3. Meri profile se mera brand: awaaz, offer, audience
Phir har reel ke liye: hook (pehle 2 sec), script (30-45 sec), on-screen text, caption, comment keyword.
Pehle 30 din ka plan table mein dikhao. Meri approval ke bina kuch bhi schedule ya post mat karna.
Step 3

Review karo, phir schedule

  • Plan table padho: koi topic repeat to nahi, koi claim galat to nahi.
  • Pehli 3 reels poori check karo (hook, captions, sound).
  • Sahi lage tab bolo: "Theek hai, schedule kar do."
💡 Done-for-you (optional, paid): Ootto mein Done-for-you on karne pe wo har mahine 30 reels bhejne ka offer deta hai, har din ke liye ek. Ye unki service hai: pricing, terms aur sample reels unki site pe dekho, phir decide karo.
Checklist

Checklist

  • Instagram Professional account hai
  • Ootto connector add kiya, "Connected" dikh raha hai
  • Step 2 wala prompt chalaya, 30 din ka plan mila
  • Pehli 3 reels khud check ki
  • Approve karke hi schedule kiya
