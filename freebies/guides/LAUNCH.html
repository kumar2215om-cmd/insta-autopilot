5 places to launch your app (with links + tips)

Thanks for commenting LAUNCH! Post your app in these 5 places first, one at a time, not everywhere on the same day.

1 · Reddit2 · Hacker News3 · Product Hunt4 · Indie Hackers5 · UneedChecklist
1 · Reddit

1 · Reddit r/SideProject

reddit.com/r/SideProject

  • Write it like a builder: what it does, who it's for, and "would love honest feedback". Reply to every comment.
  • Read the sub's rules first, and don't post the same link across 10 subs at once.
2 · Hacker News

2 · Hacker News (Show HN)

news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html

Title format
Show HN: [App name] – [what it does in plain words]
  • Link to something people can try without a long signup.
  • Hitting the front page is never guaranteed, but when it happens, thousands of people can see it in a day.
3 · Product Hunt

3 · Product Hunt

producthunt.com

  • Launches go live at 12:01 AM Pacific time (about 12:30 PM in India).
  • Tue–Thu: most traffic, but also the toughest competition. Weekends: fewer launches, so it's easier to rank. Pick based on your goal.
  • Prepare a tagline, 3–5 screenshots or a short demo video, and a first comment explaining why you built it.
4 · Indie Hackers

4 · Indie Hackers

indiehackers.com

Share your story and numbers (what you built, why, first results). Founders here try new tools and give feedback.

5 · Uneed

5 · Uneed

uneed.best

  • Only a few products launch per day, so you don't get buried. Every launch gets a lifetime backlink to your site.
  • The free option puts you in a waiting queue; paid lets you pick your date.
Checklist

Launch-week checklist

  • One clear sentence: what your app does and for whom
  • A landing page that works on mobile and has an email or signup capture
  • 3–5 screenshots or a 30-second demo
  • Spread the launches over 1–2 weeks so you can reply to everyone
  • Write down every piece of feedback, then fix the top 3 issues
⚠️ Visitor and vote numbers in the reel are examples, not promises. Platform rules and pricing can change, so check each site before posting.
