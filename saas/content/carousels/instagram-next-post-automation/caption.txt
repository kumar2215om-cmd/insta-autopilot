How to set up Instagram auto DMs before you post

→ To have auto DMs ready before you post, create the automation for your next post instead of a specific reel.
→ Auto DM → Create → Comments on your post or reel → Next post.
→ Add the keyword, replies and DM.
→ Publish the reel whenever you like; the automation moves onto it automatically.
→ Or turn on auto-setup: write "Comment GUIDE" in the caption and ReplyInbox creates the automation from your template.
