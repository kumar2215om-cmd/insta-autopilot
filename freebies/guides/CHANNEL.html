14 commands

Kaunsa command kab

Video banane se pehle

/youtube ideate10 ranked video ideas, har ek ka hook angle + thumbnail concept
/youtube scriptRetention ke hisaab se script: hook, intro, content blocks, har 60–90 sec pe pattern interrupt, CTA
/youtube hook5 alag tarah ke hooks + kis pe log drop ho sakte hain
/youtube thumbnailThumbnail brief: 3 A/B variants, colour hex codes, composition
/youtube shortsShorts script with visual-change markers + loop setup

Upload ke time

/youtube seo3 title options, description, tags, chapters, hashtags
/youtube metadataCopy-paste upload package: title, description, tags, chapters, end screens

Plan + grow

/youtube strategyPositioning, content pillars, upload cadence, 30/60/90 din ke milestones
/youtube calendarPoore mahine ka content calendar + Shorts plan
/youtube competitorCompetitors ke top videos, keyword gaps, format gaps
/youtube auditChannel health check: SEO, performance, content, monetization
/youtube analyzeAnalytics samjhata hai: retention graph, traffic sources, kya fix karo
/youtube monetizeKamai ke 7 raste, brand deal rate card, 90-din plan
/youtube repurposeEk video se Shorts, LinkedIn post, X thread, email, blog outline
7-din plan

Naya Hinglish channel? Ye 7 din follow karo

  1. Din 1: /youtube strategy chalao. Niche, audience aur 3 content pillars final karo.
  2. Din 2: /youtube competitor se apne niche ke 3 channels ke gaps nikalo.
  3. Din 3: /youtube ideate se 10 ideas lo, top 4 chuno.
  4. Din 4: Pehle idea ke liye /youtube hook + /youtube script.
  5. Din 5: /youtube thumbnail brief se thumbnail banao (Canva / AI image tool).
  6. Din 6: Record + edit, phir upload se pehle /youtube metadata.
  7. Din 7: /youtube repurpose se usi video ke 3 Shorts / Reels + 1 LinkedIn post.
💡 Har hafte ye cycle repeat karo. Mahine ke end mein /youtube calendar se agle mahine ka plan.
ready prompts

Copy karo, brackets bharo

channel setup
/youtube strategy
Mera channel: [naam]. Niche: [jaise AI tools for students]. Language: Hinglish. Audience: [age, city tier, kya chahte hain]. Main hafte mein [2] videos + [3] Shorts bana sakta hoon. Mera goal 6 mahine mein [10K subscribers / pehli kamai].
Hinglish script
/youtube script
Topic: [video topic]. Length: [8 min]. Hinglish mein likho (Hindi + English mix, Devanagari nahi, Roman). Pehle 15 second mein strong hook, beech mein ek real example, end mein comment CTA.
Reels bhi isi se
/youtube repurpose
Is script se 3 Instagram Reels banao (har ek 30–45 sec): hook line, on-screen text, aur ek comment keyword CTA.
