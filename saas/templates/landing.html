Questions

FAQ

Which accounts work?

Instagram Business or Creator accounts (free to switch in Instagram: Settings → Account type and tools).

Is it safe for my account?

Yes. {{ brand }} uses Instagram's official API, approved by Meta. We never ask for your password, and you can disconnect any time.

How does the AI DM agent work?

It answers DMs that aren't one of your keywords, using only your FAQ, products and links, in the language the fan writes in. It never makes up prices or links, and anything like refunds or brand deals is handed to you with a suggested reply. You can switch it off any time.

Why do people tap a button before getting the link?

Instagram only lets apps check whether someone follows you after they reply to your DM. The button tap is that reply.

What are Instagram's limits?

An app can DM someone who commented once per comment, within 7 days. Follow-ups must go within 24 hours of the person's last message. Very busy reels can hit Instagram's hourly limit; {{ brand }} then waits and continues.

When can I buy Pro?

Pro is launching soon. Start on Free today: everything you set up stays when you upgrade.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Invite up to 5 people as Manager or Viewer from Settings. They don't need your Instagram password.
