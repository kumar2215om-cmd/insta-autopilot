Connect ChatGPT to Instagram with Ootto

Thanks for commenting GPT! The full setup from the reel: Ootto connected to ChatGPT as an MCP app, so you can audit your reels and create new ones from a chat.

Before you start

Read this first

⚠️ This uses Ootto (ootto.ai), a third-party tool, connected to ChatGPT as an MCP app. Menu names in ChatGPT change often. If you don't see Plugins, look under Settings → Apps & Connectors (you may need to turn on Developer mode under Advanced). Check pricing and plan limits on ootto.ai before you connect your account.
What it does

What it does

  • Audit: looks at your page and shows where viewers dropped off on your last reel, and why it may not have gone viral.
  • Create: makes reels for you on autopilot, from inside ChatGPT.
Setup

Setup (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open Plugins: in ChatGPT, open Plugins in the left sidebar.
  2. Add: press Add (top right) and choose Create MCP App.
  3. Fill the form (values below). Tick I understand and want to continue, then press Create.
  4. Connect: ChatGPT asks you to connect Ootto. Press Continue to ootto.
  5. Sign in to Ootto, or sign up if you're new. You land back in ChatGPT with Ootto connected. No API key to copy.
  6. Ask: open a new chat and type @ootto create reel. That's one message.
