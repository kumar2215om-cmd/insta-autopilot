Not legal advice

⚠️ Not legal advice. These are mainly US and EU laws (COPPA, CAN-SPAM, California CIPA and auto-renewal law, DMCA, GDPR) and the figures are maximums ("up to"). If you have users there, get a lawyer to review. India's DPDP Act also applies to Indian users (see the RULES checklist).
The prompt

The prompt

Paste into Claude Code
You are auditing this app for 6 common legal risks in vibe-coded apps. For each one: (1) find where it applies in this codebase, (2) explain the risk in one line, (3) implement the fix, (4) list anything I must do manually outside the code.

1. AGE / COPPA: Signup has no age check. Add an age or date-of-birth gate; block under-13s (or route to verifiable parental consent); don't store data for blocked users. Add a line about children in the privacy policy.

2. GOOGLE FONTS / GDPR: Fonts load from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Self-host all fonts (download the woff2 files into /public/fonts, use @font-face or next/font local), and remove every request to Google font servers.

3. SESSION REPLAY / WIRETAP: Find analytics tools with session replay or keystroke capture (PostHog, Hotjar, LogRocket, FullStory, Clarity, etc.). Turn replay off by default, mask all inputs, and only enable it after explicit consent from a cookie/consent banner. Add a consent banner if none exists (Accept / Reject / Manage; non-essential off until accepted).

4. EMAIL / CAN-SPAM: Every marketing email template must include a working one-click unsubscribe link (plus List-Unsubscribe headers), the business's physical postal address, and an honest subject and from-name. Respect unsubscribes immediately.

5. SUBSCRIPTIONS / AUTO-RENEWAL: Next to every subscribe or checkout button, clearly show the price, billing interval, that it renews automatically until cancelled, and how to cancel. Get affirmative consent (unchecked checkbox), send a confirmation email with these terms, and make online cancellation easy (Stripe customer portal link).

6. USER UPLOADS / DMCA: If users can upload images or content, add a /dmca (copyright) page with a takedown process and a designated agent contact, add upload terms (users own and license their content), and add a report button on user content.

Also add or update: privacy policy, terms of service, and a footer with links to both.

At the end, give me a checklist table: item | status (fixed / needs manual action) | file(s) changed.
Manual

Manual steps the code can't do

  • Register a DMCA designated agent with the US Copyright Office (online, a small fee; renew every 3 years) if you host user uploads and have US users.
  • Add your real postal address (a business address or PO box) to email settings.
  • Have a lawyer review the privacy policy and terms if you're serious about the US or EU market.
  • Re-test signup, emails, checkout and the cookie banner after the changes.
Self-check

Quick self-check (2 minutes)

  • Open your site with DevTools → Network, and search "fonts.g". Should be 0 requests.
  • Sign up with age 12. Should be blocked.
  • Send yourself a marketing email. Unsubscribe and an address should be in the footer.
  • Look at the checkout button. Renewal terms should be right next to it.
