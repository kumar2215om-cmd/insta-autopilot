The AI social media system: ideas, calendar, hooks

Before you start

Honest note

⚠️ AI does the thinking and drafting fast, but you still decide, record and post. Most people save about half their planning time. Your results depend on your niche and consistency.
Step 0

Set up your brain (one time)

Create a Project in Claude (or a custom GPT) and paste this as the project instructions:

Project instructions
You are my social media strategist.
Brand: <name> | Niche: <niche> | Audience: <who, age, city, language>
Goal: <followers / leads / sales>
Tone: <e.g. Hinglish, friendly, short lines>
What worked before: <your top 5 posts + views>
Always answer in tables when planning. Be specific, no generic advice.
💡 Upload your best captions or scripts as files so it learns your voice.
Steps 1–3

Ideas, calendar, hooks

1 · Content ideas
Give me 30 content ideas for my niche.
Mix: 40% educational, 30% relatable/story, 20% proof/results, 10% promo.
Table: # | idea | angle | why my audience cares
2 · 30-day calendar
Turn these ideas into a 30-day calendar.
Table: date | topic | format (reel/carousel/static/story) | hook | CTA keyword
Don't repeat the same topic within 7 days.
3 · Hooks + format
For idea #<n>, write 10 hooks (first 3 seconds, max 8 words each).
Then tell me the best format for it and why.
Steps 4–5

Captions, design + reel script

4 · Captions
Write the caption for post #<n> in my tone.
Structure: 1-line hook, 3–5 short value lines, 1 CTA, 8–10 hashtags.
5 · Design + reel script
Give me a slide-by-slide carousel outline (max 7 slides) for post #<n>.
Write a 30–45 sec reel script with on-screen text for every line.
💡 Use Canva for design, and CapCut or Claude Code + Remotion to edit.
Steps 6–8

Timing, comments, analytics

6 · Best time to post. Open Instagram → Insights → Total followers → Most active times, take a screenshot, and upload it:

6 · Best time
From this screenshot, give me the best 2 posting slots for each day of the week.
7 · Comments
Here are 20 comments from my last reel. Group them into questions, objections and praise.
Write short replies in my tone, and suggest 3 new content ideas from the questions.

8 · Analytics (why reach dropped). Screenshot the post insights (reach, watch time, skip rate, shares, saves) and upload them:

8 · Analytics
Compare this post with my top 3 posts. Why did reach drop?
Check: hook, first 3 sec retention, length, topic, posting time.
Give me 3 specific fixes for the next post.
Routine

Weekly routine (about 2 hours)

DayWhat to do
MondayIdeas, calendar and hooks for the week (steps 1–3).
TuesdayCaptions and scripts (4–5), then record and edit.
DailyPost at your best slot, then reply to comments (7).
SundayRun the analytics review (8) and feed the learnings into next week.
