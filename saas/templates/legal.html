Privacy policy

{{ brand }} helps Instagram creators reply to comments and send direct messages automatically. This policy explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram account: account ID, username, name, profile picture, follower count and an access token from Instagram's official login. We never see your password.
  • Your settings: keyword rules, reply texts, DM texts and links you write.
  • Activity: the last 100 automation events (for example the commenter's username, the comment text and whether a DM was sent), so you can see what happened. Older events are removed automatically.
  • Counts: how many DMs, replies and links were sent.
  • Contacts: the Instagram usernames of people who interact with your automations, and the email address or phone number they choose to send in the DM when your automation asks for it, or through the signup form on your link page. Only you can see and export them.
  • AI Inbox (only if you turn on AI replies): the last 40 messages of each DM conversation, so the AI can answer in context and you can read them in the inbox.

How we use it

Only to run the automation you set up: reading comments and messages on your account, replying, sending DMs and checking whether someone follows you before sending your link. We don't sell data, show ads or use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

On Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (database), service providers that process data on our behalf. If you turn on AI replies, the DM conversation and the details you give the AI (your FAQ, products and links) are sent to Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model, to write each reply. Anthropic processes it on our behalf and doesn't use it to train its models.

Your choices

Use Disconnect Instagram and delete my data in the dashboard, or remove {{ brand }} in Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). Either way we delete your account data. See Data deletion.
