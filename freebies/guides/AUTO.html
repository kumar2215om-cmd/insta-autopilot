Claude + Instagram: setup + prompts

Kya milega

Claude aapke Instagram ke saath

Claude ke built-in Connectors mein Instagram nahi hai, isliye ye setup ek third-party connector (Ootto) se hota hai. Connect hone ke baad Claude aapki profile, top posts aur reach padh ke batata hai kya chal raha hai, competitors dekhta hai, aur mahine bhar ka content plan banata hai.

⚠️ Zaroori: zyaadatar Instagram tools ke liye Professional account (Creator ya Business) chahiye. Ootto ki pricing aur terms use karne se pehle khud check karo.
Setup

Setup (2 minute)

  1. Instagram app mein check karo ki account Professional hai (Settings → Account type and tools).
  2. claude.ai kholo, Customize pe jao, phir Connectors tab.
  3. Add custom connector dabao.
  4. Name: Ootto. Remote MCP server URL: Ootto ki official website se link copy karke paste karo.
  5. Add, phir Connect dabao, Instagram se login karo.
  6. Permissions padho, phir Allow. Connectors list mein "Connected" dikhe to done.
💡 Custom connectors har Claude plan pe available nahi hote; option na dikhe to apna plan check karo.
Prompts

4 ready-made prompts

Pehle confirm karo ki Claude bole ki wo Ootto se connected hai. Phir ye prompts ek-ek karke chalao; brackets mein apni details bharo.

1 · Reach audit
Meri Instagram profile ke last 30 posts aur reels padho. Reach, saves, shares aur watch time ke hisaab se top 5 aur bottom 5 nikalo. Batao top posts mein kya common hai (hook, topic, length, posting time) aur reach kahan gir rahi hai. Answer table mein do.
2 · Competitor research
Mere niche [topic] mein 5 aise pages dhoondo jo mujhse thode bade hain. Har ek ke last 10 reels ke hooks, formats aur posting frequency compare karo. 3 cheezein batao jo wo kar rahe hain aur main nahi.
3 · Monthly content calendar
Upar ke audit aur competitor research ke basis pe agle 30 din ka content calendar banao. Har din: format (reel/carousel/story), topic, pehle 3 second ka hook, caption ka idea, aur comment keyword. Table mein do. Kuch bhi post mat karna.
4 · DM reply drafts
Mere pending DMs dekho aur unke reply drafts likho, mere tone mein (friendly, Hinglish, short). Sales ya personal sawaalon pe draft mat likho, unhe mere liye mark karo. Kuch bhi send mat karna; main padh ke approve karunga.
Safety

Safety: account ki chaabi

Aap ek third-party app ko apne Instagram ka access de rahe ho, to ye rules follow karo.

  • Permissions padho: Allow se pehle dekho app kya kar sakta hai (posts padhna, DMs bhejna, comments).
  • Approval pehle: prompts mein likha hai "send/post mat karna". Auto-send kabhi on mat karo jab tak replies khud check na kar lo.
  • Robotic DMs = unfollow: same copy-paste reply sabko mat bhejo; short, personal aur honest raho.
  • Spam rules: bahut zyada automated DMs ya comments se Instagram account restrict kar sakta hai. Dheere chalo.
  • Access hatana ho to: Instagram mein Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites se Ootto remove karo. Claude mein Customize → Connectors se disconnect karo.
