Before you start

Honest note

⚠️ ₹10,713 was my real collected amount over 7 days (before fees). Results vary. This works only if the ebook genuinely solves a problem.
Step 1

Pick a topic

Pick a specific problem people already pay to solve (save money, get a job, learn a skill, fix health or habits, grow a business). "My life story" doesn't sell. "Excel for beginners in 7 days" does. The list is below.

Step 2

The Claude prompt

Replace [TOPIC] and [READER] and paste it into Claude:

Copy karo
You are an expert author and teacher. Write a practical ebook on [TOPIC] for [READER, e.g. complete beginners in India].
Structure:
1. A strong title + subtitle (give 5 options, pick the best)
2. Introduction: the problem, who this is for, what they will be able to do after reading
3. 7 chapters, each with: a clear explanation, a real-world example (Indian context where it fits), common mistakes, and 3 action steps
4. A quick-start checklist and a 7-day action plan
5. A short conclusion
Rules: simple language, short paragraphs, no filler, no exaggerated claims. Mark any fact or number I should verify with [CHECK]. Write chapter by chapter. Start with the outline and wait for my OK.
Step 3

Design it in Gamma

  1. Go to gamma.app, choose Create new → Paste in text, and paste Claude's text.
  2. Pick a document-style theme, add a cover, then Export → PDF.

Before you sell (don't skip)

  • Read it fully once
  • Check every [CHECK] fact and remove anything you can't verify
  • Add your own examples, screenshots or templates. This is what makes it worth paying for
  • Add a short "who this is NOT for" line to avoid refunds
