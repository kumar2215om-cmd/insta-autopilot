Your products

Add each thing you sell, then paste its sales link into your payment page as the page people land on after paying.

How to connect your payment page
  1. Open your payment page's settings (for example Razorpay Payment Pages or Instamojo) and look for "Redirect after payment" or "Thank-you page URL".
  2. Paste the product's sales link from above and save.
  3. Put your payment page's link in your Auto DMs as usual. {{ brand }} counts the click, and the sale when the buyer lands on the sales link.
  4. Optional: add ?order=ORDER_ID if your payment page can pass the order number, so a refresh is never counted twice.

No redirect option on your payment page? Clicks and UTM tags still work; only the ₹ numbers need the redirect.

Where buyers come from (UTM tags)

Your website, Shopify or Google Analytics then shows which reel each visitor came from, e.g. ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=prompts-reel. Links that already have UTM tags, and automations with click counting off, are left as they are.
