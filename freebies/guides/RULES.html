Website compliance checklist before May 2027

Before you start

Not legal advice

⚠️ Not legal advice. This is general information. Have a lawyer review it for your business. The DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified Nov 2025) apply in phases, with most duties in force by around May 2027. Points 5 and 6 come from consumer and e-commerce rules, not the DPDP Act.
Why now

Why now

If your site has a contact form, signup, checkout, WhatsApp opt-in or a tracking pixel, you're collecting personal data. Failing to protect it can mean penalties of up to ₹250 crore under the DPDP Act.

1–2

Privacy notice + consent

1 · Privacy notice / policy

  • Lists exactly what data you collect (name, phone, email, location, cookies…)
  • Why you collect each item (specific purpose)
  • How long you keep it, and when it's deleted
  • Who you share it with (payment gateway, courier, CRM, ad platforms)
  • How users can access, correct or delete their data and withdraw consent
  • Written in clear, simple language (English plus a local language is better)

2 · Consent

  • No pre-ticked boxes. Consent needs a clear action.
  • Separate consent for marketing (WhatsApp, SMS, email offers)
  • Withdrawing is as easy as giving (an unsubscribe link, a toggle, "STOP")
  • Keep a record of when and how consent was given
3–4

Grievance contact + cookies

3 · Grievance officer / contact

  • Name (or role) plus email or phone, clearly visible on the site
  • A simple process to respond to complaints within a fixed time

4 · Cookies & tracking

  • A cookie or tracking banner that tells visitors what you track (analytics, Meta pixel, Google Ads)
  • Accept, Reject and Manage options, with non-essential tracking off until accepted
5–7

Terms, selling online, children

5 · Terms & conditions

  • The rules of your service: payments, cancellations, liability, disputes

6 · If you sell online

  • Return, refund and shipping policies
  • Legal business name, address and contact details on the site

7 · Children's data

  • If users can be under 18, get verifiable parental consent
  • No tracking or targeted ads aimed at children
8

Security & breach response

  • HTTPS / SSL on every page
  • Strong admin passwords plus 2FA, regular updates and backups
  • Access limited to the people who need it
  • A breach plan: inform affected users and the Data Protection Board without delay

Also don't forget

  • Delete data you no longer need (set retention periods)
  • Keep simple records of processing and consents
  • Contracts with your developer and vendors (see the CONTRACT checklist)
Prompts

Claude prompts

Privacy notice
Write a clear privacy notice for <business> (India) that collects <data list> for <purposes>, shares with <vendors>, keeps data for <period>. Follow the DPDP Act 2023 notice requirements. Simple English + Hindi summary.
Consent + cookie banner
Write consent text and a cookie banner for my website: separate checkboxes for marketing, Accept / Reject / Manage options, and an easy withdraw option.
