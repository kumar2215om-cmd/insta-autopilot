FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? {% if contact_email %}Email us{% else %}Write to us{% endif %}, we're happy to help.

Which accounts work?

Instagram Business or Creator accounts. Switching is free in Instagram: Settings → Account type and tools.

Is it safe for my account?

Yes. {{ brand }} is built on Meta's official Instagram API. We never ask for your password, and you can disconnect any time.

How does the AI DM agent work?

It answers DMs that aren't one of your keywords, using only your FAQ, products and links, in the language the fan writes in. It never makes up prices or links. Refunds, brand deals and anything it's unsure about are handed to you with a suggested reply.

Why do people tap a button before getting the link?

Instagram only lets apps check whether someone follows you after they reply to your DM. The button tap is that reply.

What are Instagram's limits?

An app can DM someone who commented once per comment, within 7 days. Follow-ups must go within 24 hours of the person's last message. Very busy reels can hit Instagram's hourly limit; {{ brand }} then waits and continues.

When can I buy a paid plan?

Creator, Pro and Agency are launching soon. During the beta every feature is free. Start today: everything you set up stays when you upgrade, and yearly plans give you more than 2 months free.

Can I manage more than one Instagram account?

Yes. Log in with each account once, then switch between them from the sidebar. Each account has its own automations, inbox and contacts.

Is there a phone app?

Yes. Open {{ brand }} on your phone and install it from Settings (Android: Install app; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). Turn on notifications to hear about new leads right away.

Can I message all my followers at once?

Instagram only allows messages to people who DMed you in the last 24 hours, so Broadcast sends to them. That's still everyone who reacted to your latest reel or story.

How does it know which reel made a sale?

Add your product in {{ brand }} and paste its sales link into your payment page (Razorpay, Instamojo and others) as the page buyers land on after paying. The sale is then credited to the last DM link that buyer tapped. We never see their payment details.

What if something goes wrong with my account?

You get a notification if Instagram logs you out, starts refusing or slowing DMs, or you're close to your monthly DM limit, so automations never stop silently.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Invite up to 5 people as Manager or Viewer from Settings. They don't need your Instagram password.
